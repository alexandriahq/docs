Ambient is distributed through organization-specific rollout channels. Use the installer link your organization sent you.

## Set up Ambient

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your organization-specific installer">
    Download links are intentionally not published in these docs. If you cannot find yours, contact the person managing your rollout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install and sign in">
    Launch Ambient and sign in with the account assigned to your organization. Ambient may install or connect its Bridge companion during setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant system access">
    Allow the permissions required for the sources your organization uses. Depending on your platform and settings, this can include screen recording, microphone, accessibility, or input monitoring.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose capture sources">
    Open **Settings** and review the enabled sources. Screen, microphone, system audio, keyboard activity, active app/window, and accessibility context can be controlled independently when available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add exclusions">
    Exclude private apps and websites before you begin. Ambient also applies built-in exclusions and platform protections such as secure-input handling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm recording">
    The sidebar capture card should say **Capturing**. If it says **Paused**, press **Start**. If it is blocked, follow the permission or sign-in message shown in the card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to expect during rollout

- Your organization controls which Ambient features are available.
- Recording, **Activity**, and **Memory** can remain available when Agents, Skills, or Reports are disabled.
- Some deployments use recorder mode: the device captures approved raw sources and uploads them for organization-managed processing instead of showing local derived results.
- Ambient may show a calibration notice during the first 24 accumulated hours of active recording.

<Warning>
  Only record work approved by your organization. Review capture sources and exclusions whenever your work context changes.
</Warning>
