The sidebar capture card is the quickest way to understand and control recording.

## Pause and start recording

When Ambient is recording, press **Pause** below the capture card. A normal pause waits until you press **Start** again and survives an app restart.

Use the arrow beside **Pause** for a timed pause:

- **For 15 minutes**
- **For 1 hour**
- **Until tomorrow morning** — resumes at 08:00 local time

While paused, the card shows when recording will resume. Press **Start** at any time to resume immediately. Your individual source settings stay unchanged, so Ambient resumes only the sources that were previously enabled.

You can also control recording from the Windows/Linux tray, the macOS Dock menu, the live-recording control, or **Settings → Capture**. A timed pause resumes after sleep or after reopening Ambient if its resume time has passed.

<Note>
  Permission problems, sign-out, setup, or an update can block recording independently of your Start/Pause choice. Follow the message in the capture card when **Start** does not resume capture.
</Note>

## Review Activity

Open **Activity** to see processed threads built from recent recorded work. A thread groups related observations over time. Activity can continue to work even when assistant features are disabled for your organization.

The capture card distinguishes local recording from processing. If it says **Capturing locally** or **Processing paused**, recording may still be continuing while derived Activity is delayed.

## Export activity

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Activity">
    Wait for the threads you need to appear. Load earlier threads first if you want them included.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Export">
    Select **Export** in the Activity header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select threads">
    Ambient selects the currently loaded threads by default. Check or uncheck individual threads; one export can include up to 200.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the JSONL file">
    Choose **Export selected threads**, then select where to save it. Ambient shows progress and lets you cancel before completion.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each exported thread contains a metadata record followed by its ordered observations and any handoff proposals. The export contains readable processed activity text and may contain sensitive information. It does not include screenshot or audio files.

<Warning>
  Review exported files before sharing them. An export can include activity captured when redaction was disabled.
</Warning>
