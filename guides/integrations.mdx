Open **Settings** to review capture before recording sensitive work.

## Capture controls

Ambient can use sources such as screen, microphone, system audio, keyboard activity, active app/window, accessibility context, and approved agent conversations. Availability depends on platform, organization policy, and system permissions.

Turning off a source stops future capture from that source. The master **Start/Pause** control stops or resumes all enabled sources without changing the individual toggles.

## Exclusions

Add apps and websites that Ambient should skip. Built-in exclusions and platform protections still apply. Check exclusions whenever you change browsers, applications, or work environments.

## Storage and processing

- Standard deployments keep capture artifacts and processed context locally and use remote services only as configured for processing and integrations.
- Local files and exported backups are not currently described as encrypted by Ambient.
- Recorder-mode deployments can upload approved raw capture sources to organization-managed infrastructure for later processing. On-device exclusions still run before an artifact exists.
- Uploading in recorder mode does not immediately delete the local artifact; local retention and the organization rollout determine its lifecycle.

## Connected services

If enabled, integrations can provide read-only context from services such as Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, Todoist, Granola, or Toggl Track. Integrations do not automatically enable recording sources, and recording permissions do not automatically connect integrations.

<Warning>
  Your organization determines its capture, processing, sharing, and retention policy. Ask your rollout owner when a setting or data destination is unclear.
</Warning>
