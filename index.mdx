Ambient records work on your computer so your organization can understand how real tasks get done.

During the current rollout, the main experience is recording: choose approved capture sources, pause whenever you need privacy, review recent activity, and export selected timelines. Your organization may hide assistant features such as Agents, Skills, or Reports.

## The recording workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install from your rollout channel">
    Use the organization-specific link shared with your team. Public documentation does not contain product download URLs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what Ambient can record">
    Grant the requested system permissions and configure capture sources and exclusions in **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the capture card visible">
    The sidebar shows whether Ambient is capturing, paused, starting, blocked, or waiting for processing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and export">
    Open **Activity** to review processed threads. Use **Export** to save selected recorded timelines as JSONL.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Ambient calibrates during roughly the first 24 hours of accumulated recording time. Paused time, sleep, and time while the app is closed do not count.
</Note>
